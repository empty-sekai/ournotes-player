ournotes-player 0.2.0

Live charts, Live2D models, stories and UI packs resolve shared resources from nnnotes.product/1 manifests. Typed object accessors supply textures, materials, meshes, prefabs, animations and shaders to the renderers.

Story products support language-specific UI and fonts, shared models and media, home and live-result hosts, and silent playback. Cue volume routing uses CRI categories; missing cues complete as silence. Chart listings offer live variants, and the validation and read-set tools understand the product graph.

Compatibility:
- Story and UI products use the formats generated by nnnotes 0.3.x.
- Format 2 chart sites and format 2 Live2D model sites remain supported.
- Node.js 22 or newer is required for package tools.
